## Interdisciplinary Context: Shadow Co-Author

### Cybernetics & Systems Theory
The problem of transferring tacit knowledge (M. Polanyi): how to preserve information that is not formalized and exists only in the creator's experience? The Shadow Co-Author offers protocols for transmitting context, doubts, and values — the «spirit» of the system.

### Philosophy
The problem of preserving meaning and identity after the author's death (R. Barthes, M. Foucault). The principle shifts the focus from «I must leave instructions» to «I must leave a map for interpretation», accepting that heirs will reinterpret the system.

### Psychology
The trauma of losing a key knowledge carrier in a team, the indispensability syndrome. The Shadow Co-Author reduces anxiety by documenting doubts and preparing a living carrier of the spirit — this is a prevention of both organizational amnesia and the personal fear of disappearing.
